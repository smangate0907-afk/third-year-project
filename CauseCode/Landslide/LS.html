Towards Light

We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1. Red Cross Society of China (Landslide & Mountain Disaster Relief)

This national body actively accepts public donations to deploy specialized rescue equipment, emergency medical teams, and financial compensation to families trapped or displaced by massive regional landslides.

redcross.org.cn

2. Mukul Madhav Foundation (India)

This Indian foundation actively steps in during regional terrain disasters, hill-region cloudbursts, and major slope failures (such as the devastating Maharashtra village landslides).

mmpc.in

3. Prime Minister's National Relief Fund - PMNRF (India)

India's primary official national fund which routinely allocates emergency financial relief, medical compensation, and reconstruction funds for victims affected by major landslides in vulnerable hilly zones like Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Kerala.

pmnrf.gov.in

4. Swiss Solidarity (Swiss-based International Disaster Relief)

This foundation collects public donations across Europe to fund emergency stabilization projects, search operations, and long-term rebuilding for communities devastated by sudden rockfalls and major soil-liquefaction landslides

swisssolidarity.ch

5. SEEDS India (Sustainable Environment and Ecological Development Society)

An established Indian non-profit organization that specializes in disaster response and community resilience, focusing extensively on fragile mountainous regions prone to heavy soil erosion and landslides.

seedsindia.org

6. START Network (International Landslide & Crisis Fund)

This global network pools and manages rapid-response crisis funds specifically for under-reported or sudden natural disasters like catastrophic mountain landslides and mudflows.

startnetwork.org
