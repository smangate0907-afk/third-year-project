We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1. GlobalGiving (Earthquake Relief Fund)

This global crowdfunding platform launches dedicated emergency funds whenever major earthquakes strike around the world.

https://www.globalgiving.org

2. Direct Relief (Emergency Earthquake Fund)

This humanitarian organization accepts public donations specifically to provide emergency medical resources, trauma kits, and healthcare funding during major earthquakes.

https://www.directrelief.org

3. Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF)

This is India's most trusted official fund where the public can directly donate money for natural calamities, with a major focus on earthquakes.

https://pmnrf.gov.in

4. Japanese Red Cross Society

This is Japan's primary humanitarian organization that actively accepts public donations and deploys emergency medical teams and relief supplies specifically during major earthquakes.

https://www.jrc.or.jp

5. Nepal Red Cross Society

As Nepal's leading disaster response organization, it accepts international and public donations specifically for earthquake preparedness, rescue, and rehabilitation.

https://www.nrcs.org

6. GiveIndia (Disaster & Earthquake Relief Campaigns)

India's leading donation platform that activates verified fundraising campaigns specifically when severe earthquakes hit India or neighboring regions.

https://www.giveindia.org
