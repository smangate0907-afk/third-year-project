Towards Light

We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1. American Red Cross (Hurricane & Cyclone Disaster Relief)

This major organization runs massive emergency operations during catastrophic hurricanes and coastal cyclones. They accept public donations to operate emergency shelters, distribute food supplies.

redcross.org

2. ActionAid Association (India - Cyclone & Storm Relief)

ActionAid India plays a critical frontline role during major coastal cyclones in states like Odisha, West Bengal, and Andhra Pradesh (such as past cyclones Amphan and Fani).

actionaidindia.org

3. Prime Minister's National Relief Fund - PMNRF (India)

India's primary official national fund which allocates heavy financial assistance, medical compensation, and reconstruction aid for citizens affected by severe natural disasters, particularly devastating coastal cyclones.

pmnrf.gov.in

4. Goonj (India - Disaster Relief & Cyclone Support)

Goonj is a well-known Indian non-profit organization that handles large-scale material and financial relief during major natural calamities, including severe cyclones hitting eastern and southern coastal belts.

goonj.org

5. Americares (Emergency Cyclone & Storm Response)

Immediately steps in when major cyclones and typhoons strike vulnerable coastal areas. They accept public funding to deliver emergency medicines, medical supplies, and mental health support to survivors trapped or injured in storm-ravaged regions.

americares.org

6. Global Empowerment Mission (GEM)

GEM is an international disaster relief organization that actively deploys to regions struck by severe tropical storms, hurricanes, and coastal cyclones.

globalempowermentmission.org
