UNITED24

UNITED24 is the official fundraising platform of Ukraine, created to collect donations for humanitarian, medical, reconstruction, and other recovery needs.

National Bank of Ukraine

The National Bank of Ukraine is the country's central bank and provides official information about Ukraine's financial system, banking sector, and economic support mechanisms.

https://bank.gov.ua/en/
Razom for Ukraine

Razom for Ukraine is a nonprofit organization working with partners to provide humanitarian assistance, emergency support, and other aid to communities affected by the war in Ukraine.

https://www.razomforukraine.org/
Nova Ukraine

Nova Ukraine is a nonprofit organization supporting humanitarian relief and community recovery in Ukraine, with assistance focused on people affected by the ongoing crisis.

https://novaukraine.org/
Ukrainian Red Cross Society

The Ukrainian Red Cross Society provides humanitarian assistance and emergency support to people affected by conflict and other crises, including relief, healthcare, and community assistance.

https://redcross.org.ua/en/
Caritas Ukraine

Caritas Ukraine provides humanitarian assistance, social support, emergency relief, and recovery programmes for vulnerable people and communities affected by the war.

https://caritas.ua/en/
International Rescue Committee (IRC) — Ukraine

The International Rescue Committee supports people affected by the war in Ukraine through humanitarian assistance, healthcare, protection, economic support, and other essential services.

https://www.rescue.org/country/ukraine

Voices of Children provides psychological and psychosocial support to children affected by war, helping children and families cope with the consequences of conflict and displacement.
