Towards Light

We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1.Direct Relief (Emergency Flood & Tsunami Fund)

This international humanitarian organization actively accepts public donations to deliver emergency medical assistance, clean-up supplies, and survival kits specifically during devastating floods and coastal tsunamis worldwide.

directrelief.org

2. Hemkunt Foundation

An India-based non-profit organization that has played a frontline role during severe regional crises, particularly massive seasonal floods across states like Assam, Punjab, and Delhi.

hemkuntfoundation.com

3.Mukul Madhav Foundation (MMF)

This Indian trust actively steps in during heavy monsoon disasters, urban floods, and river overflows (such as recurring floods in Assam, Punjab, and Maharashtra).

mmpc.in

4.Aktion Deutschland Hilft (Germany)

This is Germany's primary national relief coalition that pools emergency funds specifically for catastrophic floods, heavy storms, and tsunamis. They accept public donations across Germany

aktion-deutschland-hilft.de

5.ShelterBox

This international disaster relief charity specializes entirely in emergency shelter and tools for families left homeless by natural disasters, particularly severe monsoons, river floods, and tsunamis.

shelterbox.org

6. Global Empowerment Mission (GEM)

GEM is a well-known international disaster relief organization that focuses heavily on sudden-onset calamities like major floods and hurricanes.

globalempowermentmission.org
