We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1.. Palestine Red Crescent Society (PRCS)

Operating as an official independent humanitarian society within the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, PRCS manages emergency medical services, ambulance corps, and hospitals in Palestine (Gaza and West Bank).

https://www.globalgiving.org

2.. UNRWA (United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees)

UNRWA is the primary UN agency providing vital healthcare, emergency food distribution, education, and crisis cash-assistance to millions of registered Palestine refugees caught in active conflict zones across Gaza, the West Bank, and surrounding regions.

https://www.directrelief.org

ActionAid India participates in international humanitarian relief appeals, extending support to vulnerable populations displaced by severe geopolitical conflicts and humanitarian emergencies.
