Towards Light

We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1. UN Women (United Nations Entity for Gender Equality and the Empowerment of Women)

UN Women is the global champion for gender equality, working extensively to end violence against women and girls, protect their rights, and empower them worldwide.

donate.unwomen.org

2. Save the Children (Global Child Protection & Welfare)

Operating globally across more-than 100 countries, Save the Children is a premier international organization focused entirely on defending children from abuse, exploitation, child labor, and dangerous conflicts.

www.savethechildren.org

3. Polaris Project (USA - Human Trafficking & Child Exploitation Prevention)

Polaris is a leading U.S.-based organization that fights against human trafficking and modern-day slavery, which disproportionately traps women and children.

polarisproject.org

4. Child Rights and You - CRY (India)

CRY is India’s leading and most trusted non-profit organization dedicated to ensuring child rights, safety, education, and protection from child labor, child marriage, and trafficking.

www.cry.org

5. Kailash Satyarthi Children’s Foundation - KSCF (India)

Founded by Nobel Laureate Kailash Satyarthi, KSCF works internationally and nationally to end child labor, child trafficking, child sexual abuse, and to promote girl-child education and safety.

satyarthi.org.in

6. Breakthrough India (Women & Girl Child Safety)

Breakthrough is an India-based human rights organization working on the frontlines to end violence against women and girls, prevent early marriages, and challenge gender-based discrimination.

in.breakthrough.tv
