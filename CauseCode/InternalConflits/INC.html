Towards Light

We only suggest trusted, official and verified websites

1. International Committee of the Red Cross - ICRC (Global Civil Conflict Relief)

The ICRC operates globally to protect and assist victims of internal armed conflicts, civil wars, and domestic violence. They accept international public donations to deliver emergency medical care, surgical treatment

www.icrc.org

2. Médecins Sans Frontières / Doctors Without Borders (MSF)

MSF delivers emergency medical humanitarian assistance to people affected by internal conflicts, civil unrest, and violence where healthcare infrastructure has collapsed.

www.msf.org

3. National Defence Fund - NDF (India)

Established to accept voluntary public contributions, the NDF heavily finances the welfare, medical rehabilitation, and educational needs of dependents of security personnel who face hostile engagements etc.

ndf.gov.in

4. International Rescue Committee (IRC - Internal Displacement Support)

The IRC responds globally to humanitarian crises caused by violent civil conflicts and political upheavals. They accept public donations to help people whose lives have been shattered by internal warfare to survive.

www.rescue.org

5. Prime Minister's National Relief Fund - PMNRF (India)

India's premier official national fund which routinely allocates immediate financial assistance, medical treatment compensation, and ex-gratia relief to victims and families affected by domestic communal riots, internal civil disturbances, and violent clashes.

pmnrf.gov.in

6. Khalsa Aid (Internal Conflict & Riot Relief - India & Global)

Khalsa Aid is an international humanitarian NGO that actively steps into domestic civil conflict zones and riot-hit regions to provide emergency humanitarian aid.

khalsaaid.org
